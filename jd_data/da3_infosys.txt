Job Description
We are looking for a Data Architect to own the end-to-end data architecture strategy across our enterprise data platform.
Define and document the enterprise data model, data policies, naming standards, and governance rules that determine how data is collected, stored, secured, and consumed across business units.
Design scalable, high-performance logical and physical data models and schemas that support integration, storage, and retrieval across multiple domains.
Own and maintain ER diagrams, data dictionaries, and metadata standards across all systems.
Define data security, data retention, backup, and disaster recovery policies aligned with compliance requirements.
Establish architectural standards for data warehousing, master data management, and cross-team data integration patterns.
Evaluate and select appropriate database technologies (relational, NoSQL, columnar) for new initiatives, balancing cost, performance, and long-term maintainability.
Lead architecture review boards and provide technical guidance to data engineering and analytics teams on schema design and platform choices.
Partner with engineering leadership to translate business requirements into long-term data platform roadmaps.
Assess current data architecture for gaps, technical debt, and scalability risks, and propose remediation strategies.
Required Skills:
Proven experience architecting enterprise-scale data platforms and data warehouses.
Deep expertise in data modeling (conceptual, logical, physical) and tools such as ER/Studio or similar.
Strong understanding of data governance, data quality frameworks, and master data management.
Experience defining architecture standards across AWS data services (S3, Glue, Redshift, Lake Formation) or equivalent cloud platforms.
Excellent stakeholder management and the ability to present architectural decisions to both technical and business audiences.
AWS certification(s) such as AWS Certified Data Analytics or Solutions Architect is a plus.
